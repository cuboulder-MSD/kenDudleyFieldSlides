# Ken Dudley Field Slides
## Description
These slides were taken by Ken Dudley of the Institute of Arctic and Alpine Research’s (INSTAAR) 1969 and 1970 field seasons. The field locations were on the Broughton and Baffin Islands in Canada which is the home of the Inuit Nunangat and Nunavut tribes. Dudley (1920 -1994) was an art teacher in Toronto, Canada. While traveling in the Canadian Arctic region in 1969, he met John Andrews on Broughton Island. Dudley then became INSTAAR team’s translator after they found he knew the Inuktitut language. The next year he came back with his son, Jon Dudley, and traveled again with the team.
